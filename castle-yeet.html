How to Play

Click and drag enemies to grab them, then fling them into the sky to destroy them. Build a Temple first, then drop enemies on your castle to convert them into troops!

Catch enemies while they are falling, then throw them again for CATCH AND RELEASE. Throw the same enemy again for DOUBLE YEET and TRIPLE YEET.

Build your YEET combo with AIR MAIL, BOWLING, CASTLE DELIVERY, and GIANT KILLER. Skilled throws earn bonus money and charge your ULTIMATE YEET.

Archers, Wizards, and Bombers all come from converted enemies. Assign them to towers between waves.

Fill the YEET bar with skilled throws. When ULTIMATE YEET activates, grab one enemy to pick up a nearby cluster, then throw the whole pile.

When the wave timer ends, spawning stops, but remaining enemies must still be defeated.

Between waves, assign your converts to castle towers, each building has different defensive abilities. Survive as many waves as you can!
